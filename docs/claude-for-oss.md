# Claude for Open Source: eligibility and evidence guide

Anthropic's [Claude for Open Source program](https://claude.com/contact-sales/claude-for-oss) offers eligible open-source maintainers and contributors six months of Claude Max 20x. This page summarizes the [official terms](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-for-oss-terms) and helps applicants present verifiable evidence. It is not affiliated with Anthropic, cannot certify eligibility, and cannot guarantee approval.

Criteria can change. This guide was checked on **2026-07-29**; read the official page and terms immediately before applying.

## Apply once, through the canonical form

The shortened `t.co/Pe5DWayYB7` URL and the `claude.com/contact-sales/claude-for-oss` URL lead to the same program. Do not submit both. Anthropic says concurrent or duplicate applications may be disregarded, and resubmitting does not speed up review.

Applications are for natural persons, not repositories or companies. Sign in with the GitHub account whose public work supports the application.

## Eligibility checklist

You must meet at least one Maintainer Track criterion **or** make a credible Ecosystem Impact case, and you must meet every general requirement.

### Maintainer Track — one is enough for consideration

- Projects you maintain have, in aggregate, at least **500 dependent repositories** or **100 dependent packages**.
- Packages you maintain have at least **200,000 combined monthly downloads** across public registries.
- You are a listed committer or maintainer of a recognized foundation or language project.
- You authored at least **100 pull requests merged into public repositories you do not own** during the preceding 12 months.
- A repository you maintain had at least **20 unique external contributors with merged pull requests** during the preceding 12 months.
- A repository you maintain has an [OpenSSF Criticality Score](https://github.com/ossf/criticality_score) of at least **0.4**.

### Ecosystem Impact Track

If none of those thresholds fits, the terms allow a discretionary application for work the ecosystem meaningfully depends on. Anthropic says it may consider downstream dependents, breadth of usage, functional criticality, and your maintenance role. Developer tooling is one of its examples.

Stars and social impressions are not named eligibility metrics. They may help explain reach, but they do not replace evidence of dependence, substantive contribution, or critical function.

### General requirements — all are required

- You are a natural person and at least 18, or the age of majority where you live.
- You live where Claude.ai is available and are not restricted by applicable export or sanctions rules.
- Your GitHub account is in good standing and at least two years old.
- You have public open-source commits, pull requests, reviews, or releases within the preceding 90 days.
- You maintain or contribute to a project under an OSI-approved license.
- You are not an Anthropic employee, contractor, agent, or their immediate family or household member.

Meeting a threshold makes an application eligible for consideration; it does not promise approval. Anthropic says it also evaluates the nature, substance, and quality of the work.

## Evidence worksheet for the 500-word explanation

Use public, dated links. Replace every bracketed prompt with facts; delete anything that does not apply.

```text
Qualifying path
I am applying under [exact Maintainer criterion / Ecosystem Impact]. As of
[date], [registry or GitHub/OpenSSF URL] reports [exact metric and window].

My role and substantive work
I am [maintainer/core contributor] for [project URLs]. In the relevant period I
[released/reviewed/implemented/triaged] [specific work], evidenced by [links].

Ecosystem significance
[Who] depends on this work for [critical function]. Public examples include
[dependent repositories, packages, integrations, or organizations with links].

How I would use Claude Max
I would use it for [maintenance tasks such as issue triage, compatibility
research, test design, security review, contributor support, or documentation].
This would help me [specific, observable maintenance outcome].
```

Before submitting, check that the GitHub account email can receive the activation message and that the selected repository is the clearest evidence for the chosen path. Anthropic may also inspect public registry data, GitHub's dependency graph, and OpenSSF data.

## Evidence skillcheck contributors can preserve

For work on this project, useful public evidence includes substantive merged pull requests, reviews, release and issue triage, maintained language packs or subsystems, real downstream integrations, npm downloads, GitHub dependents, and dated project releases. [MAINTAINERS.md](../MAINTAINERS.md) records formal project responsibility.

Contributing here does not automatically qualify anyone. Do not split work into artificial micro-PRs, create bot activity, trade engagement, purchase stars or downloads, or otherwise inflate a threshold. Anthropic's terms say trivial, duplicative, automated, purchased, or manipulated activity may be discounted and can lead to disqualification or revocation.

The strongest application is the simplest one: name the exact path, link the evidence, explain your real role, and describe a concrete maintenance use for the subscription.
